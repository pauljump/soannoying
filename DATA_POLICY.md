# Data and Source Material Policy

This repository combines project-authored annotations with short excerpts and pointers to third-party public material.

- The project releases its original code and documentation under the MIT License.
- The project makes no ownership claim over third-party source material. Rights remain with the relevant authors and platforms.
- Source excerpts are kept short, attributed by source pointer, and included so claims can be reviewed in context.
- Reusers are responsible for complying with applicable platform terms, copyright, privacy, and data-protection obligations.
- Do not treat the data as containing consent from source authors for profiling, training, identification, or commercial contact.

For removal or correction requests, open a repository issue without reposting sensitive content, or contact the maintainer through GitHub.
