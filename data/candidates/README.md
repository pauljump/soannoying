# Screened candidates

These are the result of a corpus-level Big Annoyance Test pass over the v0.1 observations and 270 labeled mining-run rows.

They are **screened candidates**, not reviewed problem records. The source links show why each pattern is worth a closer look. The wording is a working description, not a claim that the problem is common or solved.

The compiler merged overlapping rows and kept patterns that looked repeated, costly, hard to work around, and broader than one isolated product complaint. It used Codex `gpt-5.5` at low reasoning effort over the labeled mining rows and the earlier candidate set. A later delta review added `techsupport` and `apple` coverage, removed weak or narrow patterns, and kept 31 candidates. It did not use the provisional `fixability` label as evidence. Every retained candidate has at least two exact source URLs.

The next step is community review: merge overlapping candidates, reject weak ones, add better evidence, and only then create records in `data/problems/`.
